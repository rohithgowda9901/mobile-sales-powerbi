# 📱 Mobile Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Mobile Sales Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard analyzes mobile sales performance across brands, products, cities, months, payment methods, customer ratings, and other business dimensions to identify trends and generate actionable business insights.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into an interactive business intelligence solution that supports data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer key business questions such as:

- What is the overall sales performance?
- Which mobile brands generate the highest sales?
- Which products contribute the most to revenue?
- Which cities have the highest sales?
- How do sales change across months?
- Which payment methods are most commonly used?
- What are the customer rating patterns?
- How does sales performance vary over time?

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Apple** generated the highest sales among the analyzed brands.
- **iPhone SE** was one of the top-performing mobile models.
- **Delhi** recorded the highest sales among the analyzed cities.
- Sales showed noticeable variation across different months.
- **UPI and Debit Card** were among the major payment methods used by customers.
- Customer ratings provide additional insight into overall customer satisfaction.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Focus inventory and promotional strategies on high-performing brands and models.
2. Investigate sales patterns in high-performing cities for regional expansion opportunities.
3. Use monthly sales trends to improve inventory and promotional planning.
4. Encourage popular digital payment methods to provide a smoother customer experience.
5. Monitor customer ratings to identify opportunities for improving customer satisfaction.
